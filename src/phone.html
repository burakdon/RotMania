ROTMANIA

A hot-seat strategy game about building the thing that ends you.

Premise

A whistleblower from Frontier Lab Inc. says the world ends on January 1, 2030. You run a nation. AI deployments make you rich and push a shared track toward a hidden line. Cross it and you sank the 8-ball: you lose, the game ends, everyone else scores.

Years 2023–2029. Each year: an event, two actions per nation, then winter production (which spreads the AI). Pass the phone when told. Keep your intel to yourself.
